# intro

cofounded RocademySTL w/ Ethan Carter (@ UChicago), a hands-on coding camp built to teach students real game development through building projects in the roblox platform, not lectures.

it was created after noticing there weren’t any strong, modern coding camps in the local area that actually focused on practical skills and real-world building.

## impact so far

- **$6,000+** earned in total revenue
- **15+** students served, over 2 years
- **$1,000+** given in merit aid (partnered with launchcode)

## what students do 

students learned the fundamentals of lua coding & blender. collaborated

## goal

to make coding feel practical, fun, and accessible so students leave with real skills and finished projects, not just notes.
